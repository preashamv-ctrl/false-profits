    The Meaning Behind The Name

    FALSE PROFITS

    A Call to Integrity & Purpose

    The name carries a deliberate double meaning: Profits (the endless worldly race for money and validation) and Prophets (the outside voices speaking doubt or deception over your destiny). Culture constantly tells you to compromise who you are for a quick return. Meanwhile, doubters, self-proclaimed gatekeepers, and cynicism line your path—acting as modern false prophets trying to talk you out of the dreams God placed in your spirit. False Profits is our refusal to trade our soul for worldly noise.

    01 Silence Outside Noise

    Never allow someone who didn’t give you your calling to talk you out of your destiny. We build modern streetwear as armor for dreamers pursuing purpose with pure hearts.

    02 Uncompromising Craft

    Faith is never an excuse for mediocrity. We source 460–500 GSM loopback cottons, custom dye in neutral earth tones, and engineer silhouettes comparable to top luxury houses.
