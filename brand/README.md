## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Ink | `#08080a` | Page background, deep fills |
| Slate | `#111114` | Panels, bars |
| Field | `#1a1a20` | Inputs, elevated surfaces |
| Edge | `#26262e` | Borders, dividers |
| Bone | `#f7f6f2` | Primary text / light surfaces |
| Bone 2 | `#eeece5` | Secondary light |
| Mute | `#7d7d8c` | Secondary text |
| Gold | `#c5a059` | Accent, CTAs, rules, badges |
| Gold light | `#dec38b` | Hover / highlight |
| Gold dark | `#a3803e` | Pressed / deep gold |

Gold is accent only — never flood backgrounds with gold.

## Typography
- **Display:** Cinzel (wordmarks, heroes, section titles). Wide tracking on headlines.
- **Body:** Inter (UI, paragraphs, forms).
- **Labels:** IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (badges, micro-labels).

## Logo / wordmark
- Wordmark: **FALSE PROFITS** in Cinzel, tracking ~0.35em.
- Tagline lockup: **Faith • Integrity** under wordmark (small, mute or gold).
- Dual meaning: reject hollow *profit*; refuse false *prophets*. Do not flatten to one reading in headlines.
- Prefer bone wordmark on ink; gold sparingly for accent marks / prayer nav.

## Imagery
- FoG editorial × Generation Clo product grid energy.
- Product frames: **3:4**. Heroes: full-bleed cinematic (16:9 sources OK).
- Dark + muted gold light; no busy backgrounds under Cinzel type.
- Sample assets: `/workspace/false-profits/assets/`

## Voice (visual)
Quiet luxury, reverence, restraint. No loud discount styling. Prayer / sanctuary moments stay calm — gold as candlelight, not neon.
